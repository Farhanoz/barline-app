Common questions

How do I keep my iPhones in sync?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device and leave Settings → iCloud Sync on in Barline. Changes reach your other devices through your private iCloud.

How do I restore Barline Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Settings → Barline Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing members get it too.

How do I save workouts to Apple Health?

Settings → Apple Health → Save Workouts to Health. Each finished workout is saved as strength training with estimated calories.

How do I back up or export my workouts?

Settings → Your Data. Export Workouts (CSV) opens in Numbers, Excel, or Google Sheets; Export Backup makes a file Barline can restore. Barline also backs up automatically on your iPhone once a day.

I deleted something by mistake.

Settings → Your Data → Backups lists the automatic backups from the last month. Restoring one replaces your current data, after saving what you have now, so you can always go back.
